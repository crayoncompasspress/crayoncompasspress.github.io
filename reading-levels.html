What is a decodable book?

A decodable book only uses words a child can sound out with the letter sounds they have learned, plus a few taught "tricky" words. That means a beginning reader can succeed on their own instead of guessing from pictures. Each of our storybooks lists its new words at the back.

How to use the storybooks

  • A grown-up reads the short chapter intro aloud.
  • Your child reads the story pages alone. Help by saying each sound, then sliding them together.
  • Talk about the story with the questions at the end. About 10 minutes a day is plenty.
Start with the workbook if your child is still learning letter sounds. Start with a storybook if they can already blend simple words.
