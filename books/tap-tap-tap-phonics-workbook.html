About this book

Tap, tap, tap! Something is inside the spotted egg...

Pip the pup finds an egg in a pile of leaves, and out pops Dot, a baby dinosaur who needs to get home to her Mom. Your child helps her get there by reading. Every chapter teaches a few new letter sounds, then ends with a story page your child reads alone, using only the sounds they have learned so far.

Every word can be sounded out. The only exceptions are 4 tricky words (a, the, is, to), each taught on its own page with the tricky part marked, and the names of the 4 Pencil Rangers, which always appear next to their picture.

Inside this book:

  • Listen & Point warm-ups with no pencil needed: rhymes, first sounds, robot talk, counting and clapping sounds
  • 31 letter sounds in a gentle order, starting with s a t p i n: every letter, plus ck, qu, ch, sh, th and ng
  • Sound buttons, blending slides and follow-the-path word chains that show your child how to slide sounds into words
  • Tap it, then write it and read it, does the picture match? pages
  • 6 chapters of one real story, each ending on a cliffhanger so your child wants to read on
  • A Big Review, a Level check and a Super Reader certificate
  • A one-line tip for grown-ups on every page, a short guide to saying the sounds, plus answers

Short, playful pages built on systematic, step-by-step phonics. About 10 minutes a day is plenty. Large 8.5 x 11 inch pages. Best with crayons or pencils.

For kids ages 4 to 6 who are just starting to read, at home, in preschool or kindergarten, or for homeschool. Level 2 brings blends like st, fr and nd.

Quick answers for grown-ups

What age is this phonics workbook for?

Kids ages 4 to 6 who are just starting to read.

Is it decodable?

Yes. Every word can be sounded out with the sounds taught so far, except 4 tricky words (a, the, is, to) and the names of the Pencil Rangers.

How long should we practice each day?

About 10 minutes a day is plenty.

Do I need to know phonics to teach it?

No. Every page has a one-line tip for grown-ups, and there is a short guide to saying the sounds.

What comes next?

Read the color storybook Tap! Tap! Tap! (Level 1), then Flap! Flap! Flap! (Level 2) for blends.
