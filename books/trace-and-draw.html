About this book

Help the Pencil Rangers! A pre-writing adventure for ages 3–5

Ollo the Owl, Bibbo the Bear, Fennix the Fox and Tuki the Turtle need a helper. Every page is a little game: make the rain fall, sail a boat across the river, climb a mountain, find a way out of the cave, and earn 8 Ranger Badges along the way. Your child builds pencil control and early writing confidence one fun step at a time.

Inside this book:

  • 100 activity pages, plus a badge map and a Super Ranger certificate
  • Starts easy: wide "crayon roads", big dots, smiley start points, arrows and finish flags
  • Builds step by step through lines, curves, loops, zig-zags and diagonals
  • Shapes to trace, copy and draw on your own
  • Mazes and follow-the-path games for visual tracking
  • Numbers 1–10: trace, count, color and draw
  • Capital letters A–Z grouped by how they are made, starting with the easiest straight-line letters
  • Coloring pages and dot-to-dots starring the Pencil Rangers crew

Large 8.5 x 11 inch pages give little hands plenty of room. Best with crayons or pencils.

A screen-free gift for preschool, pre-K and kindergarten prep, for home or the classroom.

Quick answers for grown-ups

What age is this pre-writing workbook for?

Kids ages 3 to 5: preschool, pre-K and kindergarten prep.

What will my child practice?

Pencil control step by step: lines, curves, loops, zig-zags and diagonals, then shapes, numbers 1-10 and capital letters A-Z.

Is it too hard for a 3-year-old?

It starts easy, with wide crayon roads, big dots, smiley start points and finish flags, and gets a little harder each section.

Does my child need to know letters first?

No. Letters come near the end, after the tracing, shape and maze pages.

Are there more Pencil Rangers books?

Yes: Dino Dot-to-Dot Quest (ages 4-7) and Christmas Gift Quest (ages 3-6) feature the same four ranger friends.
