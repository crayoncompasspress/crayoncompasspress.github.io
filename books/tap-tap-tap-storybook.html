About this book

Tap, tap, tap! What is inside the spotted egg?

Pip the pup finds an egg in a pile of leaves, and out pops Dot, a baby dinosaur who wants her Mom. With help from the four Pencil Rangers, a bus ride, a yak in a big curly wig and lots of nibbling, Dot finds her way home.

A real story your child can read. In each of the 6 chapters a grown-up reads a short intro, then your child reads the story pages alone. Every word can be sounded out with the Level 1 sounds (short vowels, consonants, ck, qu, ch, sh, th, ng and double letters). The only exceptions are 4 tricky words: a, the, is, to.

Inside:

  • A big full-color picture on every page
  • Large, clear letters in a font made for beginning readers
  • A how-to-read-together page for grown-ups
  • Talk-about-it questions and a word list for each chapter

This is the read-alone story from the workbook Pencil Rangers Learn to Read, Level 1: Tap! Tap! Tap!, now as a color storybook. Use it after the workbook as a reward, or on its own with a child who knows their letter sounds. For ages 4 to 6.

Quick answers for grown-ups

What age is this storybook for?

Kids ages 4 to 6 who know their letter sounds.

Is it a decodable book?

Yes. Every word can be sounded out with the Level 1 sounds, except 4 tricky words: a, the, is, to.

Do I need the workbook too?

No. It works on its own, or as a reward after the Tap! Tap! Tap! phonics workbook.

Is it in color?

Yes, with a big full-color picture on every page.

What comes next?

Flap! Flap! Flap! (The Storybook, Level 2) practices blends like fl, st and gr.
