About this book

Can you crack the case before the whole school glitches?

Gearby, the friendly robot at Maplewood Elementary, has started acting very strange. Lunch trays go to the wrong kids, the bell rings backward, and a fake photo lands on the front page of the school paper. Leo and Ava need a sharp detective to help them solve 8 cases and find out who has been feeding Gearby bad data.

What's inside

  • 100 puzzles that tell one big mystery: logic grids, secret codes, pixel pictures, robot sudoku, pattern rules, "program the robot" paths, debugging challenges and spot-the-fake puzzles.
  • Gentle difficulty steps: Trainee, Junior, Senior and Master Detective levels, with how-to pages first and a hidden hint on every puzzle.
  • Clue letters in every chapter that lead to the Final Case.
  • Step-by-step answers that explain how to solve each puzzle, so kids can check their work on their own.
  • 6 two-player paper games to play with a friend, plus a Robot Detective certificate.

Why parents like it

  • Screen-free fun that builds logic, focus and step-by-step thinking.
  • A real story with funny characters, not a pile of random puzzles.
  • A simple, true lesson about AI: computers learn from examples, so good data matters.

Great for road trips, rainy days, classrooms and stocking stuffers.
