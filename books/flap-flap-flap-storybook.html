About this book

Flap, flap, flap... Flop! Can a baby flyer learn to fly?

A new egg hatches in Pip's bed, and out pops Fluff, a baby flyer who can't fly yet. Pip, Dot and the four Pencil Rangers set off to get him home to his mom's nest on the tall cliff, past a sticky bog, a windy campsite and one very cross skunk.

A real story your child can read. In each of the 6 chapters a grown-up reads a short intro, then your child reads the story pages alone. Level 2 practices blends (flap, stop, grab, jump, splash), -ing and nk words, and easy two-part words like sunset and picnic, plus a few tricky words (I, you, are, said, no).

Inside:

  • A big full-color picture on every page
  • Large, clear letters in a font made for beginning readers
  • A how-to-read-together page for grown-ups
  • Talk-about-it questions and a word list of the new words in each chapter

The sequel to Tap! Tap! Tap! (The Storybook, Level 1). For children who know their letter sounds and are ready for blends. For ages 4 to 7.

Quick answers for grown-ups

What age is this storybook for?

Kids ages 4 to 7 who know their letter sounds and are ready for blends.

What phonics skills does it practice?

Blends like fl, st, gr and nd, -ing and nk words, two-part words like sunset and picnic, and the tricky words I, you, are, said, no.

Should we read Level 1 first?

It helps. Level 2 builds on the sounds in Tap! Tap! Tap! (Level 1), but any child who knows letter sounds and short vowels can start here.

Is it in color?

Yes, with a big full-color picture on every page.

How does it look on a tablet?

It is a fixed-layout Kindle book, so each page looks like the printed page.
