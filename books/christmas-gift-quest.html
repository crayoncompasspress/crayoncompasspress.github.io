About this book

Santa's sleigh is broken, and the gifts still need delivering!

Join Ollo the Owl, Bibbo the Bear, Fennix the Fox and Tuki the Turtle on a five-stop Christmas quest: the Snowy Forest, the Frozen Lake, Santa's Toy Workshop, Candy Cane Village and finally Christmas Eve. Kids color a gift on their quest map after every stop!

Inside this book:

  • Shaped mazes: find the way through a mitten, a star, a snowman, a Christmas tree, a Santa hat and more
  • Dot-to-dot puzzles with a clue on every page and big, clear numbers
  • Tracing paths and pencil-control warm-ups: icicles, ornaments, ribbons, snowy hills and swirls
  • I Spy, what comes next, matching, counting and spot-the-difference puzzles
  • Draw the other half and color-by-number pictures
  • Coloring pages, design your own sweater and stocking, and a letter to Santa
  • A Holiday Helper certificate at the end

Builds counting, focus, early pattern skills and pencil control. Large 8.5 x 11 inch pages. Best with crayons or pencils.

Quick answers for grown-ups

What age is this Christmas activity book for?

Kids ages 3 to 6: preschool, pre-K and kindergarten.

What is inside?

About 100 pages of Christmas mazes, dot-to-dots, tracing, I spy, matching, counting, color-by-number and coloring pages, told as one holiday story.

Does my child need to read?

No. Each page has a short instruction that a grown-up can read aloud.

Is it a good Christmas gift?

Yes. It is a screen-free holiday activity book for home, travel or the classroom, and a fun stocking stuffer for preschoolers.

Are there more Pencil Rangers books?

Yes: My Big Trace & Draw Adventure (ages 3-5) and Dino Dot-to-Dot Quest (ages 4-7) feature the same four ranger friends.
