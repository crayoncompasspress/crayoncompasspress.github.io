About this book

Who is hiding? Connect the dots to find out!

Join Ollo the Owl, Bibbo the Bear, Fennix the Fox and Tuki the Turtle on a giant dinosaur quest. Every puzzle has a Ranger Clue, so kids can guess who is hiding before the last dot reveals the surprise. Then they color it any way they like!

Inside this book:

  • 100 dot-to-dot puzzles in 5 levels: Hatchling, Explorer, Adventurer, Dino Expert and Dino Master
  • Gentle progression from about a dozen dots up to 90+ dots, with big, clear numbers and a smiley start dot
  • 40 prehistoric creatures: T. rex, Triceratops, Stegosaurus, Spinosaurus, Mosasaurus and many more
  • Dino ID Cards after every level with fun, kid-friendly facts, sizes and how to say each name
  • 15 bonus pages: mazes, footprint match, counting, shadow match, spot the difference and design-your-own dinosaur
  • A Secret Dinosaur finale and a Dino Master certificate

Builds counting, focus, hand-eye coordination and pencil control. Large 8.5 x 11 inch pages. Best with crayons or pencils.

A screen-free gift for dinosaur fans ages 4 to 7, for home, travel or the classroom.

Quick answers for grown-ups

What age is this dinosaur dot-to-dot book for?

Kids ages 4 to 7.

How hard are the puzzles?

There are 5 levels, from about a dozen dots up to 90+ dots, with big, clear numbers and a smiley start dot.

Can a beginner do it?

Yes. The first level uses only small numbers, and the count grows level by level.

What else is inside?

Dino ID Cards with kid-friendly facts, 15 bonus activity pages, a Secret Dinosaur finale and a certificate.

Are there more Pencil Rangers books?

Yes: My Big Trace & Draw Adventure (ages 3-5) and Christmas Gift Quest (ages 3-6) feature the same four ranger friends.
